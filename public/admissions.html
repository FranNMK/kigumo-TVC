How to Apply

🏫 Walk-in Application

  1. Visit the college admissions office with your KCSE results slip/certificate and national ID/birth certificate.
  2. Fill in the application form (available at the office or download from our website).
  3. Submit the completed form with copies of your certificates.
  4. Pay the registration fee of Ksh 1,000 at any KCB Bank branch.
    Account: 1234567890, Kigumo TVC
  5. Receive your admission letter within 48 hours.
  6. Report to the college on the specified reporting date with all original documents.

🎓 KUCCPS Placement

  1. Apply through the KUCCPS student portal during the application window.
  2. Select Kigumo Technical and Vocational College as your preferred institution.
  3. Choose your desired course based on your KCSE cluster subjects.
  4. Wait for placement results from KUCCPS.
  5. Download your placement letter from the KUCCPS portal.
  6. Report to the college with the placement letter and original documents.

KUCCPS Guidance

The Kenya Universities and Colleges Central Placement Service (KUCCPS) places students in TVET institutions based on KCSE performance and cluster subject requirements. For technical courses, relevant cluster subjects include Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, and Geography. Visit the KUCCPS website for application deadlines and cluster point requirements.

Entry Requirements

Diploma Programs

KCSE Mean Grade C- (Minus) and above with relevant cluster subjects. Specific requirements vary by course.

Certificate Programs

KCSE Mean Grade D+ (Plus) and above. Some programs may accept D (Plain) with relevant subjects.

Artisan Programs

KCPE Certificate or its equivalent. Open to all with basic literacy and numeracy skills.

Computer Packages

Open to all applicants. No minimum academic requirements. Suitable for beginners and professionals upgrading skills.

International Students

Kigumo TVC welcomes students from across East Africa and beyond. International applicants must have their academic certificates equated by the Kenya National Qualifications Authority (KNQA). Student passes can be processed through the Department of Immigration. Contact our admissions office at admissions@kigumotvc.ac.ke for guidance on the international student application process.
