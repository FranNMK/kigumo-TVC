Admission Process

How to Apply

Choose the application route that works best for you. All routes lead to the same quality education.

💻

Online Application

  1. Click "Apply Online" on this page — it's free.
  2. Fill in your personal details, KCSE results, and choose your course.
  3. Upload scanned copies of your certificates and ID/Birth Cert.
  4. Submit and receive a reference number instantly.
  5. Receive your admission letter via email/SMS within 48 hours.
  6. Report on the specified date with your original documents.
🏫

Walk-in Application

  1. Visit the college admissions office with your KCSE results slip/certificate and national ID/birth certificate.
  2. Fill in the application form (available at the office or download below).
  3. Submit completed form with copies of your certificates.
  4. Receive your admission letter within 48 hours.
  5. Report on the specified date with all original documents.
🎓

KUCCPS Placement

  1. Apply through the KUCCPS student portal during the application window.
  2. Select Kigumo Technical Training Institute as your preferred institution.
  3. Choose your desired course based on your KCSE cluster subjects.
  4. Wait for placement results from KUCCPS.
  5. Download your placement letter from the KUCCPS portal.
  6. Report to the college with the placement letter and original documents.
📞

Contact Admissions

  1. Call us on 📞 0769 394 273 (Mon–Fri, 8am–5pm).

  3. Our admissions team will guide you through the process step by step.
  4. We can also schedule a campus tour so you can see the facilities before applying.
Government Placement

KUCCPS Guidance

Everything you need to know about government-sponsored placement.

The Kenya Universities and Colleges Central Placement Service (KUCCPS) places students in TVET institutions based on KCSE performance and cluster subject requirements. For technical courses, relevant cluster subjects include Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English and Geography.

Visit the KUCCPS website → for application deadlines and cluster point requirements. Government-sponsored students pay heavily subsidised fees under the TVET Fund.

International Students

Studying from
East Africa & Beyond

Kigumo TVC welcomes students from across East Africa and the world. We are committed to providing a diverse and inclusive learning environment.

International applicants must have their academic certificates equated by the Kenya National Qualifications Authority (KNQA). Student passes can be processed through the Department of Immigration.

Contact our admissions office at admissions@kigumotvc.ac.ke for a personalised guide on the international student application process.

📋 Certificate Equating

Contact KNQA to have your foreign certificates recognised before applying.

🛂 Student Pass

We assist with the student pass process through the Department of Immigration.

🏠 Accommodation

On-campus and off-campus housing options are available for international students.
