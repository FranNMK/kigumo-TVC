Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you need? Send us a message and we'll get back to you.

How much are the fees at Kigumo TVC?

Fees are KES 22,392 per module.

How do I apply to Kigumo TVC?

There are three ways to apply: through KUCCPS (search for "Kigumo Technical Training Institute"), by walking in to the college to apply in person, or online via the Apply page.

Where is Kigumo TVC located?

Kigumo Technical and Vocational College is located in Kigumo, Murang'a County, Kenya. Postal address: P.O. Box 5-10203, Kigumo.

How can I contact Kigumo TVC?

Call us on 0769 394 273 (Monday – Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM) or email kigumotvc@gmail.com. You can also use the contact form above.
