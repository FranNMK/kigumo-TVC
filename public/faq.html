Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about Kigumo Technical and Vocational College — courses, admissions, fees, and more.

About Kigumo TVC

Where is Kigumo TVC located?

Kigumo Technical and Vocational College is located in Kigumo, Murang'a County, Kenya. Postal address: P.O. Box 5-10203, Kigumo. View the map on the Contact page.

How can I contact Kigumo TVC?

Call us on 0769 394 273 (Monday – Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM; Saturday 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM) or email kigumotvc@gmail.com. You can also send a message via the Contact page.

Is Kigumo TVC accredited?

Yes. Kigumo TVC is fully accredited by TVETA (Technical and Vocational Education and Training Authority). All programmes are examined and certified by national bodies — KNEC, KASNEB, or CDACC — under Kenya's Competency-Based Education and Training (CBET) framework.

Programmes & Courses

What programmes does Kigumo TVC offer?

Kigumo TVC offers Artisan (Level 3), Craft Certificate (Level 4), Diploma (Level 5), and Higher Diploma (Level 6) programmes across seven departments: ICT, Automotive Engineering, Electrical & Electronics Engineering, Building & Construction, Business, Hospitality & Tourism, and Agriculture. Browse all programmes on the Courses page.

Are the courses at Kigumo TVC government-accredited?

Yes. All programmes are TVETA-accredited and follow the national CBET curriculum. Graduates receive certificates from KNEC, KASNEB, or CDACC — nationally and internationally recognised qualifications.

Admissions

How do I apply to Kigumo TVC?

There are three ways to apply:

  1. Online at kigumotvc.ac.ke/apply — free, takes about 10 minutes.
  2. Walk in to the college admissions office with your certificates and ID.
  3. Through KUCCPS — search for "Kigumo Technical Training Institute" during the placement window.

Is the application free?

Yes. Both online and walk-in applications to Kigumo TVC are completely free — there is no application fee.

What are the minimum entry requirements for Diploma programmes?

Diploma (Level 5) programmes require a KCSE mean grade of C- (Minus) and above with relevant cluster subjects. Certificate programmes require D (Plain) and above; Craft Certificate D- (Minus); Artisan programmes require a KCPE certificate or equivalent. See full details on the Admissions page.

When does the next intake start?

Kigumo TVC runs three intakes per year: January, May, and September. Check the Admissions page for the current intake dates and application deadlines, or call 0769 394 273.

Fees

How much are the fees at Kigumo TVC?

Fees are KES 22,392 per module. Government-sponsored students admitted through KUCCPS pay heavily subsidised rates under the TVET Fund. Download the full fee structure from the Downloads page.
