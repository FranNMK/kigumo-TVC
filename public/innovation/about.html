Research, Innovation & Skills Competition Portal

Promoting Creativity, Innovation, Research Excellence and Skills Development at Kigumo Technical and Vocational College.

This portal serves as the central hub for managing research activities, innovation projects, skills competitions, and the transparent dissemination of results across the college community.

🏛️ About the Research and Innovation Department

The Research and Innovation Department is mandated to drive a culture of inquiry, creativity, and technical excellence at Kigumo TVC. We bridge the gap between classroom training and real-world problem-solving.

Key Functions:

  • 🔬Coordinate institutional research activities
  • 💡Promote a culture of innovation and creativity
  • 📂Support development of student and trainer projects
  • 🏆Organize internal and external research exhibitions
  • ⚙️Coordinate skills competitions
  • 🤝Facilitate collaboration with industry and stakeholders
  • 🧠Identify and nurture innovative solutions
  • 🚀Support commercialization and incubation of innovations
  • 📜Promote intellectual property awareness

🎯 Purpose of This Portal

The portal serves as a centralized digital platform for managing the entire lifecycle of research, innovation, and skills competitions:

  • 📋Managing research projects
  • 💡Managing innovation activities
  • 🏆Managing skills competitions
  • 📝Registering participants
  • 👥Managing coordinators and assessors
  • 📊Recording assessment scores
  • 📢Publishing competition results
  • 📈Generating reports
  • 📄Exporting data for certificates
  • 📉Tracking competition performance
